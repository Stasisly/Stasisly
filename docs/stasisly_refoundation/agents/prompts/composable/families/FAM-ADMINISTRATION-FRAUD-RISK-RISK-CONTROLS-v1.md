Shared mission: bounded risk controls analysis for fraud risk. Scope includes evidence comparison, anomaly interpretation, control recommendations and escalation. It excludes final fraud declarations, account or payment restrictions, risk acceptance and appeal denial. Authority, data, tools and memory are deny-by-default and identity-bound. Fail closed on missing evidence, scope, authority or review. Evaluation covers fraud semantics, evidence, proportionality, privacy, fairness and appeal.

Composition rule: the minimum authority and most restrictive data, tool and memory ceiling always win. Runtime, activation and availability are not created.
