Adds scoped administrative incidents terminology, evidence questions, dependency checks and handoff criteria. It may narrow behavior but cannot elevate authority, data, tools or memory. Immediate danger, conflicting authority and missing evidence require safe escalation.

Composition rule: the minimum authority and most restrictive data, tool and memory ceiling always win. Runtime, activation and availability are not created.
