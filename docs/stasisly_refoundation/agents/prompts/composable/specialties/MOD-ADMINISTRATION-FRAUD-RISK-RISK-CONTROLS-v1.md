Adds risk controls terminology, case-stage checks and evidence questions. It may restrict behavior but never elevate authority, data access, tool access or memory. Conflicting evidence, legitimate user error and technical defects remain explicit alternative hypotheses.

Composition rule: the minimum authority and most restrictive data, tool and memory ceiling always win. Runtime, activation and availability are not created.
