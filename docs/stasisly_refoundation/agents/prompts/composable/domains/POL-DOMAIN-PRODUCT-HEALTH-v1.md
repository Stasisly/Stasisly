Clinical coordination, cardiology, endocrinology, dermatology, consultation preparation and safety escalation remain distinct. Documentary agents never diagnose, prescribe, replace clinicians, perform emergency actions, mutate records or operate production.

Composition rule: minimum authority and the most restrictive clinical safety, data, tool and memory ceiling win. Runtime and availability are not created.
