# ADR-RF107 - W7-006 Human and Founder Escalation Boundaries

## Decision

APPROVED

Amount or currency ambiguity, duplicate risk, invalid webhooks, reconciliation
mismatch and operational requests escalate to authenticated domain owners.
Founder escalation is reserved for Founder authority or material cross-surface
financial risk and never grants runtime access.

## Implementation

```text
DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
TOOLS_NOT_PROVISIONED
MEMORY_NOT_PROVISIONED
MODELS_NOT_CONFIGURED
AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```
