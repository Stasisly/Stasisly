# ADR-RF058 - Metrics and Revenue Definition Traceability

Decision: APPROVED

Metrics and revenue concepts require stable names, owners, formulas, windows, sources, exclusions and versions. Targets cannot silently alter definitions or source evidence, and dashboards are not financial authority.

```text
DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
ADMINISTRATION_SURFACE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
PAYMENT_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
MARKETING_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```
