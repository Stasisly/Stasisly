# ADR-RF118 - W7-008 Domain and Scope Boundaries

## Decision

APPROVED

Clinical coordination, cardiology, endocrinology, dermatology, consultation
preparation and clinical safety escalation remain distinct specialties in the
Product `health` domain. Guidance stays non-diagnostic and no family inherits
another family's authority or clinical scope.
