# ADR-RF041: Cross-Area Product Coherence and Transparent Agent Participation

## Status

Decision: `APPROVED`

Implementation:

```text
DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED

AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```

## Decision

Cross-area Product conclusions preserve participating agents, relevant
contributions, provenance, uncertainty and the decision route. Stasis resolves
coordination conflicts through explicit handoffs and escalation, not hidden
authority.

## Consequences

Salud, Nutricion, Entrenamiento and Wellness retain bounded ownership while the
user can understand who contributed and why.
