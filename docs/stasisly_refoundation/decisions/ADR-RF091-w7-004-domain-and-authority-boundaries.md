# ADR-RF091 - W7-004 Domain and Authority Boundaries

## Decision

APPROVED

Subscriptions, entitlements, invoices, payments, refunds, disputes,
settlements, payouts, taxes, ledgers and reconciliation are separate contracts.
Documentary agents may analyze bounded evidence but cannot execute financial
operations, approve providers, alter ledgers or exercise final authority.
