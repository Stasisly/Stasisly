# ADR-RF025: Cross-Surface Security, Privacy and Audit Independence

## Decision

`APPROVED` on 2026-07-31.

Security, Privacy, Audit and Risk functions retain independent challenge and
evidence duties across Product, Development and Administration. Implementers
cannot certify their own critical controls or suppress adverse evidence.
