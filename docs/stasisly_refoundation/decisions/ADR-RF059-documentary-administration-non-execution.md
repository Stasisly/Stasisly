# ADR-RF059 - Documentary Administration Agents Are Not Executors

Decision: APPROVED

Wave 6 prompts provide documentary analysis, recommendations and handoffs only. They do not create operational identities, access tools or memory, mutate records, spend funds, contact users, launch campaigns or execute payments.

```text
DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
ADMINISTRATION_SURFACE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
PAYMENT_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
MARKETING_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```
