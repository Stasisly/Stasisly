# ADR-RF112 - W7-007 Authority and Operational Restrictions

## Decision

APPROVED

W7-007 authority is documentary analysis and recommendation only. Exploitation,
scanning without scope, secret or key access, permission changes, production
mutation, privacy-rights decisions, attribution, incident command and external
disclosure remain with authenticated human owners.

## Implementation

```text
DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
TOOLS_NOT_PROVISIONED
MEMORY_NOT_PROVISIONED
MODELS_NOT_CONFIGURED
AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```
