# ADR-RF020: Cross-Surface Coordinator Conflict Resolution

## Decision

`APPROVED` on 2026-07-31.

Coordinator conflicts preserve evidence, apply normative layer precedence and
attempt bounded resolution. Cross-surface conflicts escalate to Nexus;
unresolved, critical or Founder-exclusive matters escalate to the Founder.
Direct critical escalation cannot be blocked by Nexus.
