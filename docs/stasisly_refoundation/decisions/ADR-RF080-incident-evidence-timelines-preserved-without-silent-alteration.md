# ADR-RF080 - Incident Evidence and Timelines Must Be Preserved Without Silent Alteration

Decision: APPROVED

## Decision

Incident evidence preserves provenance, timestamps, custody, limitations, contradictions, decisions and communications. Corrections append transparently. Fabrication, selective omission, silent deletion and timeline alteration are forbidden. Closure does not erase evidence or post-incident obligations.
