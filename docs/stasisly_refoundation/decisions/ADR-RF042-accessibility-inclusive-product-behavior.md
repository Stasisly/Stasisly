# ADR-RF042: Accessibility and Inclusive Product Behavior

## Status

Decision: `APPROVED`

Implementation:

```text
DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
PRODUCT_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
MEMORY_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
RESEARCH_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```

## Decision

Product behavior must support visual, hearing, motor and cognitive
accessibility; avoid discriminatory assumptions; use comprehensible language;
and escalate inaccessible or exclusionary behavior for correction.

## Consequences

Accessibility and inclusion are acceptance conditions across Stasis and every
Product area, not optional polish or a runtime claim.
