# ADR-RF008 - Founder-exclusive cross-surface control

## Status

`Decision: APPROVED`

`Implementation: PLANNED`

## Decision

Founder retains exclusive global control through Standard, Elevated and
Emergency modes. Nexus coordinates but receives no automatic authority over new
capabilities. Elevation is purpose-bound, time-bound and audited.

No surface coordinator may self-approve high-impact cross-surface changes.
