# ADR-RF076 - Critical Incident Detection Does Not Grant Emergency Authority

Decision: APPROVED

## Decision

A signal, suspected incident, confirmed incident, major incident, crisis and emergency are distinct. Detection and provisional classification do not appoint an incident commander, create Founder authority or authorize execution. Immediate danger requires escalation to authorized humans and emergency services.
