# ADR-RF057 - High-Impact User, Fraud and Moderation Review

Decision: APPROVED

Suspension, fraud and moderation recommendations require relevant evidence, proportionality, independent human review, notice where safe and an appeal path. Automated or agent-only final enforcement is forbidden.
