# ADR-RF132 - W7-010 Domain and Scope Boundaries

## Decision

APPROVED

Internal medicine, nephrology, immunology, infectious diseases,
informational oncology and medication information remain distinct specialties in the Product `health` domain.
Guidance stays non-diagnostic and no family inherits another family's authority
or clinical scope.
