# ADR-RF069 - Fraud Signals, Scores and Hypotheses Are Not Enforcement Decisions

Decision: APPROVED

Signals, indicators, anomalies, risk factors, hypotheses and model scores are inputs with uncertainty. They are not guilt, verified facts, decisions or authorization for enforcement. Detection, analysis, investigation, recommendation, decision, enforcement and appeal remain separate stages.
