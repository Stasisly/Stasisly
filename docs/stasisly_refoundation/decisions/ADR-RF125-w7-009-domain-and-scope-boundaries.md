# ADR-RF125 - W7-009 Domain and Scope Boundaries

## Decision

APPROVED

Gastroenterology, geriatrics, gynecology, health education, health monitoring
and hematology remain distinct specialties in the Product `health` domain.
Guidance stays non-diagnostic and no family inherits another family's authority
or clinical scope.
