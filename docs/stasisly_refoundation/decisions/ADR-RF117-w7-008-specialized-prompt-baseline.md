# ADR-RF117 - W7-008 Specialized Prompt Baseline

## Decision

APPROVED

W7-008 comprises exactly 90 Product health agents: 75 HIGH and 15 CRITICAL
across six approved families. Every agent receives an individual identity,
32-section prompt, manifest, evaluation, P0-P14 approval, ten adversarial cases
and risk-proportional review.
