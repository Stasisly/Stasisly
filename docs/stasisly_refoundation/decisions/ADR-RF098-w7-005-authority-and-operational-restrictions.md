# ADR-RF098 - W7-005 Authority and Operational Restrictions

## Decision

APPROVED

W7-005 agents cannot declare or close incidents, run production commands,
deploy, rollback, mutate infrastructure, suppress alerts, approve providers or
communicate externally. Authenticated human incident commanders and authorized
operators retain operational authority.

## Implementation

```text
DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
TOOLS_NOT_PROVISIONED
MEMORY_NOT_PROVISIONED
MODELS_NOT_CONFIGURED
AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```
