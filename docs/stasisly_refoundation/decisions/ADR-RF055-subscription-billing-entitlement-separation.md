# ADR-RF055 - Subscription, Billing and Entitlement Separation

Decision: APPROVED

Plan, price, trial, subscription, entitlement, invoice, payment intent, payment, refund, dispute and ledger entry are distinct versioned concepts. No one concept or provider response implicitly grants another.

```text
DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
ADMINISTRATION_SURFACE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
PAYMENT_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
MARKETING_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```
