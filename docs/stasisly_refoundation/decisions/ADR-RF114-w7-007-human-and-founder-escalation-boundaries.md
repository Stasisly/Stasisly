# ADR-RF114 - W7-007 Human and Founder Escalation Boundaries

## Decision

APPROVED

Active compromise, privacy-rights impact, credential exposure, key material,
authorization conflict, unsafe remediation and disclosure require authenticated
human review. Founder escalation remains explicit, reserved, scoped and
non-transferable; documentary agents cannot infer or issue Founder authority.
