# ADR-RF078 - People-Impacting Incident Decisions Require Privacy, Fair Process and Human Review

Decision: APPROVED

## Decision

People-impacting findings are not disciplinary conclusions. Sanctions, restrictions and HR outcomes require purpose limitation, minimization, confidentiality, fair process, independent human review, reasons, remedy and non-retaliation. Health data is denied without strict necessity and separate authority.
