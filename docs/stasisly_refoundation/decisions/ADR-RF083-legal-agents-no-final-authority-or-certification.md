# ADR-RF083 - Legal and Compliance Agents Cannot Provide Final Legal Authority or Self-Certification

Decision: APPROVED

## Decision

Agents may identify requirements, analyze evidence and recommend review. They cannot provide definitive legal advice, represent Stasisly, certify compliance, invent jurisdictional rules, accept critical legal risk or self-approve exceptions.
