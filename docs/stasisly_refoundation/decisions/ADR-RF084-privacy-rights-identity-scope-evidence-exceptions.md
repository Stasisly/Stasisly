# ADR-RF084 - Privacy Rights Require Identity, Scope, Evidence and Transparent Exceptions

Decision: APPROVED

## Decision

Every future privacy-rights flow requires identity verification, scoped request, jurisdiction, legal basis, affected systems and data, exceptions, deadline, owner, evidence, completion record and appeal or complaint path. This baseline executes no request.
