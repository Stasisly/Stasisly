# ADR-RF089 - Documentary Privacy and Legal Agents Cannot Execute Operational Requests

Decision: APPROVED

## Decision

Documentary approval creates no credentials, tools, memory, request execution, deletion, disclosure, regulatory notification, provider approval, legal hold or account mutation. Every operational action requires a separately approved runtime contract and authorized human control.

## Implementation

```text
DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
PRIVACY_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
LEGAL_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
COMPLIANCE_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
REQUEST_EXECUTION_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```
