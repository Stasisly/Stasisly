# ADR-RF097 - W7-005 Domain and Scope Boundaries

## Decision

APPROVED

Detection, declaration, command, diagnosis, mitigation, recovery, verification,
communication and review are separate incident contracts. Documentary agents
may analyze bounded evidence but cannot assume command, execute operations or
transfer Development authority across surfaces.

## Implementation

```text
DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
TOOLS_NOT_PROVISIONED
MEMORY_NOT_PROVISIONED
MODELS_NOT_CONFIGURED
AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```
