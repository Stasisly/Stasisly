# ADR-RF074 - Documentary Fraud Agents Have No Enforcement or Financial Authority

Decision: APPROVED

The forty W7-001 roles may analyze, document, recommend and escalate within their identity ceilings. They cannot block or close accounts, retain funds, mutate payments, reject refunds, restrict subscriptions, change permissions, deny appeals or accept critical risk. Such authority is not provisioned by prompts.

```text
Implementation: DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
FRAUD_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
ENFORCEMENT_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
TOOLS_NOT_PROVISIONED
AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```
