# ADR-RF019: Founder Authority Boundary for Coordinator Agents

## Decision

`APPROVED` on 2026-07-31.

The Founder remains the final human authority. Coordinators distinguish `MAY`,
`MAY_WITH_APPROVAL`, `MUST_ESCALATE` and `MUST_NOT`. Nexus may coordinate an
elevation request but cannot grant it, impersonate the Founder or authorize
constitutional, critical-risk or emergency powers.

## Consequences

```text
Implementation: DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
Runtime: RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
Availability: AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```
