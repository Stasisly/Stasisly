# ADR-RF100 - W7-005 Human and Founder Escalation Boundaries

## Decision

APPROVED

Suspected production impact, uncertain severity, unsafe mitigation, missing
ownership and security or privacy signals require qualified human escalation.
Reserved authority, critical cross-surface impact, permanent exceptions and
Founder-private operations require Founder escalation without automatic access.
