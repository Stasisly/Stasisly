# ADR-RF077 - Continuity and Recovery Activation Require Explicit Scoped Authorization

Decision: APPROVED

## Decision

Continuity activation, production shutdown, failover and disaster recovery require explicit, scoped, current authorization and authorized human execution. RTO and RPO values must come from approved contracts. Recovery and closure require technical validation, evidence and residual-risk ownership.
