# ADR-RF111 - W7-007 Domain and Scope Boundaries

## Decision

APPROVED

Application security, privacy engineering, supply-chain security, dependency
security, secrets management, forensics, threat modeling, cryptography,
security incident response and authorization remain distinct specialties under
the Development `security_privacy` domain. No family inherits another family's
authority or operational scope.
