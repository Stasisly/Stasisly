# ADR-RF053 - Administration Surface Separation

Decision: APPROVED

Administration is separate from Product and Development in authority, data, entry points and operations. Gerendi coordinates Administration; Nexus governs cross-surface handoffs. `admin.stasisly.com` remains a target, not an implemented surface.

```text
DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
ADMINISTRATION_SURFACE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
PAYMENT_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
MARKETING_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```
