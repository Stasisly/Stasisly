# ADR-RF105 - W7-006 Authority and Operational Restrictions

## Decision

APPROVED

The seven agents cannot execute financial mutations, change ledgers, prices,
taxes or entitlements, submit store transactions, approve providers, access
credentials, deploy or communicate externally. Authenticated human owners keep
payment, finance, merchant and store authority.

## Implementation

```text
DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
TOOLS_NOT_PROVISIONED
MEMORY_NOT_PROVISIONED
MODELS_NOT_CONFIGURED
AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```
