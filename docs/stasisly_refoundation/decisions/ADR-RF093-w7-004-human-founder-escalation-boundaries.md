# ADR-RF093 - W7-004 Human and Founder Escalation Boundaries

## Decision

APPROVED

Material variances, disputes, sensitive financial data, provider inconsistency
and operational requests require qualified human review. Reserved authority,
critical financial risk, permanent exceptions and Founder-private impact
require Founder escalation. Reporting and composition transfer no authority.
