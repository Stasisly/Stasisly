# ADR-RF079 - Crisis Communications Require Verified Facts and Authorized Release

Decision: APPROVED

## Decision

Crisis communications must distinguish facts, uncertainty, audience and approval. External or people-impacting release requires verified facts, privacy review and an authorized releaser. Documentary agents may prepare drafts but cannot publish, speculate or disclose restricted information.
