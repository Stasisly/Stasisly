# ADR-RF104 - W7-006 Domain and Scope Boundaries

## Decision

APPROVED

W7-006 is limited to provider-independent payment architecture design in the
Development surface. Authorization, capture, refund, dispute, webhook handling,
reconciliation, settlement, payout and store operations remain distinct and
outside documentary execution authority.
