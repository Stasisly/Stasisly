# ADR-RF056 - Marketing and Growth Privacy, Safety and Anti-Manipulation

Decision: APPROVED

Marketing and Growth require consent, truthful claims, age-appropriate safeguards, bounded experiments and no dark patterns or sensitive health-data targeting. Campaigns and spend require separate accountable authorization.
