# ADR-RF085 - Consent, Retention and Deletion Must Be Purpose-Bound and Versioned

Decision: APPROVED

## Decision

Consent is affirmative, scoped, purposeful, versioned and withdrawable. Retention and deletion require owners, durations, triggers, exceptions, review, mechanisms and evidence across active data, holds, archives, backups, derived data and audit records.
