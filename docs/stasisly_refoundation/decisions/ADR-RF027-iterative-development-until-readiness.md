# ADR-RF027: Iterative Development Loops Until Readiness or Real Blocker

## Decision

`APPROVED` on 2026-07-31.

Bounded Development packages iterate through test, evidence, root-cause
correction and regeneration until readiness or a verified blocker. Git remains
canonical source control; isolated runners, reviewable diffs, tests, gates and
Founder authorization protect sensitive operations.

## Consequences

```text
Implementation: DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
Development Surface: DEVELOPMENT_SURFACE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
Runtime: RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
Availability: AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```
