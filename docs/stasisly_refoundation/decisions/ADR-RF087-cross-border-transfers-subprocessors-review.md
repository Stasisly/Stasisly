# ADR-RF087 - Cross-Border Transfers and Subprocessors Require Evidence and Review

Decision: APPROVED

## Decision

Data location, access, transfer, residency, localization, processor roles and subprocessors require documented purpose, scope, regions, safeguards, retention, deletion, audit rights, incident terms, portability and qualified review.
