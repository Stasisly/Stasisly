# ADR-RF088 - Automated Scores Cannot Become High-Impact Decisions Without Safeguards

Decision: APPROVED

## Decision

Profiling, recommendation, ranking, scoring, personalization and enforcement remain distinct. A score cannot silently become a material decision; policy, evidence, proportionality, human review, explanation, contestability and audit are required.
