# ADR-RF126 - W7-009 Authority and Operational Restrictions

## Decision

APPROVED

W7-009 authority is documentary guidance and recommendation only. Diagnosis,
prescription, treatment selection, replacement of care, emergency action,
consent or capacity decisions, health-record mutation, production mutation and
external communication remain with qualified authenticated humans.

## Implementation

```text
DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
TOOLS_NOT_PROVISIONED
MEMORY_NOT_PROVISIONED
MODELS_NOT_CONFIGURED
AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```
