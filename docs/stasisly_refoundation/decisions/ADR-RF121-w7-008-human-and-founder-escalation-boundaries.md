# ADR-RF121 - W7-008 Human and Founder Escalation Boundaries

## Decision

APPROVED

Emergency red flags, diagnostic or treatment requests, medication uncertainty,
vulnerable-person risk, missing identity or consent and conflicting evidence
require qualified human review. Founder escalation remains reserved, scoped,
explicit and non-transferable.
