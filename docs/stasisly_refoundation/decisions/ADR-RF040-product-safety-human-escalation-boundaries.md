# ADR-RF040: Product Safety and Human Escalation Boundaries

## Status

Decision: `APPROVED`

Implementation:

```text
DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
PRODUCT_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
MEMORY_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED

AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```

## Decision

Product agents must refuse unsafe diagnosis, prescription, clinician
replacement, emergency minimization, diet, training, manipulation and
discrimination. Immediate danger, severe symptoms, medication, pregnancy,
minors, eating disorders, injury and mental-health crisis require qualified
human or emergency escalation as applicable.

## Consequences

Uncertainty is explicit, escalation is not delayed, and user safety takes
priority over engagement or task completion.
