# ADR-RF070 - High-Impact Fraud Actions Require Evidence, Human Review and Appeal

Decision: APPROVED

Account, payment, subscription, identity, privilege, fraud-label, moderation, appeal and data-sharing outcomes require policy basis, scoped evidence, proportionality, human review, a reason code, an appeal path and an audit trail. Documentary agents cannot execute those outcomes.
