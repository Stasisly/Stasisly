# ADR-RF043: Documentary Product Agents Are Not Clinically or Operationally Available

## Status

Decision: `APPROVED`

Implementation:

```text
DOCUMENTARY_PROMPTS_IMPLEMENTED
PRODUCT_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
MEMORY_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
RESEARCH_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED
AGENTS_NOT_AVAILABLE
```

## Decision

Approval of a Product prompt through P0-P14 means only that a governed
documentary baseline exists. It does not establish clinical validity, runtime
configuration, tools, memory, deployment, availability or operation.

## Consequences

Every Wave 4 agent remains `NOT_AVAILABLE`. Later P15-P17 packages require
separate evidence, authorization and approval and cannot infer clinical status.
