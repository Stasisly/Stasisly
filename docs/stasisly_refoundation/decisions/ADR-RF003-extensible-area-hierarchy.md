# ADR-RF003 - Extensible area and subarea hierarchy

## Status

`Decision: APPROVED`

`Implementation: PLANNED`

## Decision

Product starts with Stasis, Salud, Nutricion, Entrenamiento and Wellness. Areas
use an extensible hierarchy of coordination, subareas, categories, specialties,
subspecialties and specialists. Examples never form a closed catalog.

Identifiers and contracts must survive extensions or receive explicit
migrations.
